Yes — after 90 days of employment, you can use the staff room rate at any Ridgeline hotel, including Taos. It's 50% off the public rate, for up to 12 nights a year, subject to availability. Book through the Team Member Travel page and show your badge at check-in. Note that the staff rate can't be combined with other offers (team-member-perks.html).

For your weekend in March, just book through the Team Member Travel page, keeping availability and your annual 12-night limit in mind.
