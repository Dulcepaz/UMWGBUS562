Hi Jenna,

You currently have 8 PTO days remaining for this year.

As for carryover: you can carry up to 3 unused PTO days into next year. Any unused days beyond that 3-day limit are paid out at your regular rate in your first paycheck of January (pto-policy.html, "Carrying PTO into next year").

So if you end the year with 8 unused days, 3 would carry over and the remaining 5 would be paid out in January.
