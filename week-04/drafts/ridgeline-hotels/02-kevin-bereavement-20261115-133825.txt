I'm so sorry for the loss of your grandmother. Here's what applies to your situation:

- For the death of a grandparent, you're entitled to up to 3 paid days off (bereavement-and-family-leave.html).
- These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but must be used within 60 days of the death.
- This time is paid and does not reduce your PTO or sick balance.
- To use it, just let your supervisor know as soon as you can — no documentation is required (bereavement-and-family-leave.html).

I hope the service on Saturday goes as well as it can, and please take the time you need.
