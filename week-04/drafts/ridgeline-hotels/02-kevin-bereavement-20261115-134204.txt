I'm very sorry for the loss of your grandmother. Please accept my condolences.

For the death of a grandparent, you're entitled to up to 3 paid days off. These days don't reduce your PTO or sick balance, and they don't need to be taken consecutively, though they must be used within 60 days (bereavement-and-family-leave.html).

To arrange the time off, just let your supervisor know as soon as you can — no documentation is needed (bereavement-and-family-leave.html).

Wishing you and your family well during this time.
