Hi Kevin,

I'm very sorry for the loss of your grandmother.

For the death of a grandparent, our policy provides up to 3 paid days off for bereavement leave. These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but must be used within 60 days, and they don't reduce your PTO or sick balance. No documentation is required — just let your supervisor know as soon as you can. (Source: bereavement-and-family-leave.html)

If you need any additional time beyond the 3 paid bereavement days (for example, for travel to and from Albuquerque), that would need to come from your PTO balance. Let me know if you'd like me to check your current PTO balance for you.

Wishing you and your family comfort during this time.
