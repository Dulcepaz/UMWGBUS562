Funds Availability Policy › Check deposits

For checks deposited at a branch or a Larkfield ATM, the first $275 of the day's check deposits is available on the next business day. The rest of the day's check deposits is available on the second business day after the day of deposit.

Funds Availability Policy › Mobile check deposits

Checks deposited with the Larkfield mobile app follow the same availability as checks deposited at a branch. A member can deposit up to $5,000 per business day through the mobile app. Members should keep the paper check for 30 days after a mobile deposit, then destroy it.

Funds Availability Policy › Cash, direct deposits, and wire transfers

Cash deposited at a branch, direct deposits such as payroll, and incoming wire transfers are available on the business day they are received.

Debit Card Dispute Procedure › How much the member may have to pay

A member's share of the loss from unauthorized debit card transactions depends on how quickly they report it. If the member reports a lost or stolen card within two business days of learning about it, they pay no more than $50. If they report it later than that, they may pay up to $500. If unauthorized transactions appear on a statement and the member does not report them within 60 days after the statement was sent, they may have to pay for transactions after those 60 days in full.

Funds Availability Policy › Longer holds

Larkfield may hold check deposits for up to seven business days when the day's check deposits total more than $6,000, when the account has been open fewer than 30 days, when the account has been overdrawn repeatedly in the last six months, or when there is reason to doubt the check will be paid. The member is told about a longer hold when the deposit is made, or by mail if the hold is placed later.

Funds Availability Policy › Business days and cutoff times

A business day is Monday through Friday, except federal holidays. A deposit made before 5 p.m. on a business day is received that day. A deposit made after 5 p.m., or on a weekend or holiday, is received on the next business day.

ANSWER
Based on the funds availability policy, a paycheck deposited at a branch is treated as a check deposit (not a direct deposit), so the standard check-deposit rule applies: the first $275 of the day's check deposits is available on the next business day, and the remaining amount (here, $1,725) is available on the second business day after the day of deposit [funds-availability-policy.html].

Two things could affect this:

- Cutoff time: the deposit only counts as "today" if made before 5 p.m. on a business day. If made after 5 p.m., or on a weekend/holiday, it is treated as received the next business day, which would push back when the $275 becomes available [funds-availability-policy.html].

- Longer holds: Larkfield can hold check deposits for up to seven business days if the account has been open fewer than 30 days, has been overdrawn repeatedly in the last six months, or there is reason to doubt the check will be paid (this would also apply if total check deposits that day exceeded $6,000, which is not the case here since the deposit is $2,000). If any such condition applies, the member would be notified at the time of deposit, or by mail if the hold is added later, and the standard next-day availability of $275 would not apply [funds-availability-policy.html].

Assuming no longer-hold condition applies and the deposit was made before the 5 p.m. cutoff, the member can use $275 of the $2,000 paycheck on the next business day.
