### You

Wildframe Coffee Roaster is a specialty coffee company that sells coffee online and through monthly subscriptions. Our brand voice is warm, plainspoken, and a little funny. Our customers care about the environment. Suggest 6 new perks for customers who have been subscribers for six months or longer. Use a numbered list of exactly 6 ideas. Each idea must be one sentence with no more than 25 words.

### Claude

1. Free "Loyal Bean" tote bag made from recycled sailcloth, because your groceries deserve better than plastic bags too.

2. Early dibs on limited small-batch roasts before we post them publicly and accidentally sell out in nine minutes.

3. A permanent 10% discount, because loyalty should pay off in coffee, not just warm fuzzy feelings.

4. Free carbon-neutral shipping upgrade, so your beans travel greener while you brew guilt-free mornings.

5. Annual "Plant a Tree" certificate, since six months of caffeine addiction deserves a little reforestation karma.

6. Access to our private "Bean Nerds" newsletter, packed with farm stories, brewing tips, and questionable puns.
