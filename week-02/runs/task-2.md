### You

Wildframe Coffee Roasters is adding three new coffees to its shop. 
Our brand voice is warm, simple and a little funny. 
Use this example from our Brand Kit to create the new coffee cards. Keep the same labels in the same order. 

Example:

HUILA, COLOBIA
Roast: Medium
Tastes like: red apple, brown sugar, cocoa
Brew it: Pour-over or drip
Real talk: The coffee that converted our office's tea drinker. Mostly.

Now create one card for each of these three coffees:

1. Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia
Light roast. 
Tastes like jasmine, lemon, bargamot.
Best as pour-over. 

2. Cerrado, Brazil 
Medium-dark roast.
Tastes like peanut, milk chocolate, low acidity.
Good for espresso or French press. 

3. Chiapas, Mexico
Medium roast. 
Tastes like almond, caramel, orange peel.
Good for drip or AeroPress.

Required format: 
Create exactly three coffee cards.
Each card must use these labels in this order:
Coffee name and country
Roast:
Taste like:
Brew it:
Real talk:

Write a short, friendly, slightly funny sentence for each Real talk line.
